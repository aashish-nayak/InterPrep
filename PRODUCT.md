# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
A PHP/Laravel developer preparing for job interviews, studying a supplied English README and updated Hinglish guide.

## Product Purpose
Make interview material easier to understand through bilingual explanations, audio, self-assessed mock interviews and animated PHP execution.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Frontend only. Existing React/Vite implementation. Preserve questions, bookmarks, local progress, mock interviews, audio and source provenance. Initially one PHP/Laravel track; other tracks may be added later.

The language switch changes questions, answers and explanatory content only. Page titles, navigation, buttons and labels stay English.

## Brand Commitments
The user requested the reading-first design and code-animation approach of https://codewithanimation.in/html/document-skeleton. Adapt the cream surface, pink accent, generous typography and code/visual relationship without copying site content or branding.

## Evidence on Hand
137 bilingual questions: all 125 numbered updated-guide entries and 12 additional topics adapted from the original README. Both originals preserved in public/sources. Detailed requirements in docs/PRD.md.
